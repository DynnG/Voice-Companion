# Savi — Simple User Guide

Savi is an AI interview companion that helps you practice for job interviews. It asks questions for your target role, accepts spoken or typed answers, and provides coaching feedback.

## What you need

- An internet connection and a web browser.
- A microphone for spoken answers, or use the text input.
- Speakers or headphones to hear Savi.
- Optional: your resume, job description, portfolio, and a camera.

## Start an interview

1. Open Savi.
2. Enter your **Target Job Title**, or choose a popular role.
3. Optionally attach documents to help Savi ask more relevant questions. Drag files into the upload area or select **Browse files**. Wait until they show **Ready**.
4. Select **Start interview** and wait for Savi's first question.

The upload screen accepts PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, MD, PPT, PPTX, PNG, JPG, and JPEG files, up to **10 MB per file**. If a file cannot be read, retry it or remove it and upload another copy.

## Answer questions

- **Speak:** Select the microphone and allow browser microphone access. Answer naturally. Savi detects when you finish speaking; you can also click the microphone again to finish.
- **Type:** Enter your response in **Type your answer…** in the conversation panel, then select **Send answer**.
- Wait while Savi processes your answer and responds with the next question.
- Follow the conversation in the live transcript.

Select **Camera** if you want to use the camera and visual analysis feature. Allow browser camera access when prompted. Select it again to turn the camera off.

## Review your answers

Use **AI Notes** to review feedback on your responses. For recorded answers, the conversation panel's **Audio Playback & AI Notes** control lets you open the answer review and listen to your recording. Use **Try Answer Again** when available to practice an improved response.

## Download your interview review

When the interview finishes, Savi opens **Interview complete**, where you can review your questions, answers, and AI Notes.

Select **Download Interview Review** to save a PDF. The PDF is generated on your device. Download it before closing the completed interview or refreshing the page.

You can also use **Download Review** during an interview once you have answered at least two questions. Close that review to continue the interview.

After completion, close the review with **X** to return to setup and start a new interview.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Microphone does not work | Allow microphone access in your browser and check the selected input device. You can also type your answer. |
| Camera is unavailable | Check browser camera permission and whether another app is using the camera. |
| File upload fails | Check the file format and 10 MB limit. Retry or upload another readable copy. |
| Savi cannot start or respond | Check your connection and the displayed error message. Try again when the service is available. |
| PDF download fails | Select **Retry** in the review window. |
